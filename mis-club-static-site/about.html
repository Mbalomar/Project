About the MIS Club

Prince Mohammad Bin Fahd University · Founded 2018

Prince Mohammad Bin Fahd University campus, Al-Khobar

The MIS Club brings together Management Information Systems students to design, build, and ship real-world web and e-commerce projects — turning coursework into a public portfolio and giving members hands-on experience with the full development lifecycle.

What We Do

Each semester, MIS Club members work in small teams to design and build full-stack web applications — most often e-commerce platforms and management information systems — applying everything covered in their coursework: responsive design, database integration, authentication, validation, and security best practices. Finished projects are published here as a public portfolio.

How This Site Works

This website itself is one of those projects: a Node.js/Express application backed by a SQLite database, with an authenticated admin dashboard members can use to publish, edit, and retire project listings without touching code.

College of Business

Management Information Systems

MIS sits at the intersection of technology and business — turning data, systems, and process design into decisions organizations can act on. This site is a working example of that: a real database, real workflows, real security, applied to a real problem.
